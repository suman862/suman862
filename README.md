## About

I am an AI/ML Engineer and Applied AI Researcher pursuing a Master's degree in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Madan Bhandari University of Science and Technology (MBUST), Nepal.

My work focuses on the intersection of **AI engineering and applied AI research**. I build modular, production-oriented AI inference systems and investigate continual learning and visual memory mechanisms for computer vision.

I am particularly interested in developing scalable AI systems while exploring research problems in deep learning, multimodal intelligence, and generative modeling.

## Current Research

**Master's Thesis: Continual Panoptic Segmentation with Visual Memory Bank**  
Madan Bhandari University of Science and Technology (MBUST) · *In progress*

My research investigates visual memory mechanisms for mitigating catastrophic forgetting in continual panoptic segmentation, with an emphasis on maintaining previously learned knowledge while adapting to new semantic categories.

**Research focus:**
- Continual Panoptic Segmentation
- Incremental Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting
- Visual Memory Banks
- Visual Prompt Tuning
- Stability–Plasticity Trade-off
- Dense Visual Prediction

**Research Interests:**  
Continual Learning · Computer Vision · Multimodal Foundation Models · Large Language Models · Deep Learning · Visual Representation Learning

**Generative Modeling Interests:**  
Flow Matching · Diffusion Models · World Models
